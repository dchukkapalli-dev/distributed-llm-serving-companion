# Data Availability Statement

The data supporting this survey — namely (i) the CSV taxonomy of all 56
included serving techniques (`taxonomy.csv`; one row per technique with
fields `name`, `pillar`, `representative_system`, `primary_objective`,
`citation_key`, `cross_pillar`), (ii) the BibTeX record of every cited
work (`references.bib`; 120 entries), and (iii) the stage-by-stage
PRISMA flow counts shown in Fig. 1 (`prisma_counts.csv`) — are released
as a machine-readable companion archive licensed under CC-BY-4.0,
permanently archived on Zenodo
(DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.20423002](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20423002))
with source mirror at
<https://github.com/dchukkapalli-dev/distributed-llm-serving-companion>.
No new experimental measurements were produced; performance figures
cited in the *Comparative Analysis of Serving Frameworks* section are
drawn from the sources listed in `references.bib` and are attributed
in situ.
